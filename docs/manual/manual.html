1. Welcome

4real-audio changes the character of your guitar's own pickups and controls into those of another guitar. Play a single-coil Stratocaster and hear it respond like a humbucker Les Paul; play a Telecaster and hear a White Falcon. One plugin holds a library of eight guitars, and you switch between them with one click.

The output stays a clean guitar signal: there is no amp, cabinet or pedal inside. Put your favourite amp simulator after it (for example AmpliTube) and the guitar you chose now drives that amp.

Good to know. Pickup positions that were not recorded cannot be selected. Each guitar remembers its own knob positions. 4real-audio works from the electrical behaviour of the pickups and controls and from the recorded sound of the real guitars. It is a very close character change, but it is not a guarantee that your guitar becomes identical to the real one: the body wood, the strings and your own playing stay yours.

All guitar and brand names in this manual and in the plugin are used only to describe a sound character. 4real audio is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any guitar maker.

2. Installation

Windows (64-bit)

1
Run 4real-audio-0.9.0-Windows-x64-Setup.exe and accept the administrator prompt.
2
Choose what to install: VST3 plugin (for your DAW), Standalone app (plays without a DAW), or both (Full installation).
3
On the last page two boxes are ticked: Start 4real-audio and Open the user manual. Keep both, one of them, or none, then press Finish.
PartInstalled to
VST3 pluginC:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\4real-audio.vst3 (every VST3 host looks here)
Standalone appC:\Program Files\4real-audio\4real-audio.exe, Start menu shortcut, optional desktop icon
This manualC:\Program Files\4real-audio\4real-audio-Manual.pdf and a Start menu shortcut
"Windows protected your PC". The installer is not yet digitally signed, so Windows SmartScreen shows this blue window. It does not mean the file is damaged. Click More info, then Run anyway.

macOS (11 or newer, Intel and Apple Silicon)

Open the installer that matches your Mac: 4real-audio-0.9.0-macOS-AppleSilicon.pkg for Macs with an M-series chip (M1 and newer), or 4real-audio-0.9.0-macOS-Intel.pkg for Intel Macs (Apple menu → About This Mac shows which one you have). Press Continue and, if you want, choose Customize to tick or untick the VST3, Audio Unit and Standalone parts. They install to /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3, /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components and /Applications.

If macOS says the package cannot be opened because it is from an unidentified developer, right-click the file and choose Open, or go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and press Open Anyway.

The manual is installed to /Library/Application Support/4real-audio/4real-audio-Manual.pdf.

Updating

Run the newer installer. It replaces the old files and keeps your account and settings.

3. Quick start

1
Open your DAW and rescan plugins if it does not see 4real-audio yet. Put 4real-audio on the insert of your guitar track.
2
Press Sign in (top right of the plugin) and sign in with your 3ffect.my.id account. See chapter 5.
3
Click a guitar in the list on the left. Guitars you own show ACTIVE; others show LOCKED and pass your sound through unchanged.
4
Under MY GUITAR (SOURCE) choose the recorded guitar that is closest to your guitar and the pickup position you are playing, or choose Auto if it is offered.
5
Play. Set INPUT so the IN meter moves well without staying at the top. Move the pickup selector, volume and tone knobs on the picture like on the real guitar.
6
Use Voicing on to switch the effect off and on and compare (A/B).

4. Where to put it in your signal chain

4real-audio expects the direct, clean sound of your guitar. Put it first, before amps, cabinets and effects.

Typical order. A compressor or EQ before 4real-audio is fine, as long as it does not distort.
  • Use a clean, undistorted signal. Distortion before the plugin makes the result unreliable.
  • Keep the level healthy: peaks should not clip in your audio interface.
  • If you play through an amp simulator that has its own pickup or guitar model, turn that model off.

5. Your account and guitar licences

Every guitar in the library is licensed separately. Your licences belong to your account on 3ffect.my.id, the same account you used to buy 4real-audio.

Signing in

Press Sign in at the top right. The account window offers two ways:

WayHow
Email and passwordType your 3ffect.my.id email and password and press Sign in.
Sign in with browserRecommended if you signed up with Google. The plugin shows a short code and opens your browser on 3ffect.my.id. Log in there if asked, check that the code is the same, and approve. The plugin signs in by itself. Press Cancel browser sign-in to stop waiting. The code works once and expires after 10 minutes.

Create account and Forgot password open the 3ffect.my.id website. The first sign-in after a quiet period can take up to a minute while the server wakes up.

Activating a guitar with a serial number

If you received a serial such as 4RL-LP-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX, sign in, type it into Serial number of a guitar and press the redeem button. The guitar becomes ACTIVE on your account and can be used on all your devices. A serial works for one account only.

Devices and offline use

  • One account can be used on up to 3 devices (computers).
  • The plugin needs the internet only for signing in, redeeming a serial and refreshing the licence. After that it works offline until the date shown as licence valid until (30 days). Opening the account window with an internet connection renews it. If it expires, only guitars lock; nothing is deleted.
  • Press Refresh after buying a new guitar to see it at once. Sign out frees this device.
A locked guitar never blocks your audio: it simply passes your sound through unchanged, so a project never goes silent.

7. Playing the guitar controls

The controls on the picture are alive and behave like the real guitar: turning a tone knob down really muffles the sound, and turning a volume knob down changes the level and the way the pickup resonates.

ControlHow to use it
PickupClick a pickup on the picture to select it.
Selector switchDrag the lever to a position or click a position. Positions that were not recorded cannot be chosen.
Volume / tone knobDrag up or down, or scroll the mouse wheel. Hold Shift for fine steps. Double-click sets the knob to 10. The value (0.0 to 10.0) is shown on the knob.
INPUT / OUTPUT / INTENSITYSame mouse actions. Double-click INTENSITY returns it to 100 %.
Each guitar remembers its own knob and selector positions, and everything is saved with your DAW project.

8. Telling the plugin about your guitar (Source)

To change your guitar into another one, 4real-audio must know what your guitar sounds like first. That is the source.

Choosing a recorded guitar

In the first box pick the recorded guitar whose pickup is most like the one you are playing (for example Les Paul for a humbucker bridge pickup, Stratocaster for single coils). In the second box pick the pickup position. Check by ear: when the source is close, the result is the most natural.

Auto

If Auto is available in your version, the plugin listens to your playing and matches it to the closest recorded guitar by itself. It shows which recorded guitars it currently resembles. Pick a fixed source instead if you prefer a constant sound.

My guitar (teach it your own pickups)

If My guitar is available, a panel appears with one column per pickup position of your guitar:

1
In the column choose which recorded guitar and position that pickup is closest to.
2
Press Learn and play a few notes with that pickup selected. Learning stops by itself after 8 seconds, or press the button again to stop.
3
Repeat for your other pickup positions. The bar below each column shows how much of that pickup the plugin has learned. Clear forgets a column.

9. Voicing, level and the three big knobs

ControlWhat it does
Voicing onTurns the whole change on or off. Switch it while playing to hear your own guitar and the chosen guitar one after the other (A/B).
Volume knob changes levelOn: the loudness difference between your guitar and the chosen guitar (including what the volume knobs do) is kept, as on the real guitar. Off: that difference at the middle of the spectrum is removed, so the loudness stays about the same and mainly the tone changes.
Auto level (recommended)Helps the plugin by adjusting, slowly and only while you play, the level it analyses. Your dynamics are not squashed. Leave it on unless you have a reason.
INPUTLevel into the plugin, −24 to +24 dB. Watch the IN meter.
INTENSITYHow strongly the change is applied, 0 to 150 %. 100 % is the normal setting; lower values blend towards your own guitar.
OUTPUTLevel out of the plugin, −24 to +24 dB. Use it to match the loudness of your own guitar when comparing.

10. The Standalone app

The Standalone app is the plugin in its own window, so you can play without a DAW. Start it from the Start menu (Windows) or Applications (macOS).

  1. Open the Options button at the top left and choose Audio/MIDI Settings.
  2. Pick your audio interface as input and output device. On Windows choose an ASIO driver if your interface has one, for the lowest delay.
  3. Choose a small buffer size (for example 128 or 256 samples) for playing. Raise it if you hear crackles.
Use headphones or mute the input monitoring in your interface software. If a speaker plays your guitar into the microphone or pickups, it will howl (feedback).

11. Using it in a DAW

  • Studio One, Reaper, Cubase, Bitwig and other VST3 hosts: rescan plugins after installing, then add 4real-audio to the guitar track's inserts. In Studio One rescan from the plugin location settings.
  • Logic Pro / GarageBand / MainStage (macOS): use the Audio Unit version, under Audio FX.
  • Use a mono or stereo track. A guitar is normally mono, and when both channels are identical the plugin only processes once to save CPU.
  • Everything is saved with the project; guitar choice, knobs and source come back when you reopen it.
  • For lowest delay while recording, use a small buffer in your DAW.
Insert it before your amp simulator. Chapter 4 shows the order.

12. Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to try
The plugin does not appear in my DAWConfirm that 4real-audio.vst3 is in C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3. Rescan plugins. Restart the DAW. Reinstall with the VST3 part ticked.
The sound does not changeIs the guitar LOCKED? Sign in and check the list. Is Voicing on ticked? Is INTENSITY above 0?
A guitar is LOCKED but I bought itOpen the account window, press Refresh. Make sure you are signed in with the same email that you used on 3ffect.my.id. If you have a serial, redeem it.
"Licence valid until" has passedConnect to the internet and open the account window; it renews by itself. You can also press Refresh.
"Too many devices"An account can be used on 3 devices. Sign out on a computer you no longer use, then sign in again.
Sign-in is slow or says the server is unavailableThe account server can need up to a minute to wake up. Wait and try again. Check your internet connection. Try Sign in with browser.
The sound is distorted or cracklesLower INPUT so the IN meter does not hit the top. Increase the DAW or interface buffer size. Make sure no distortion is placed before the plugin.
The result sounds unnaturalChange the source to a recorded guitar closer to yours, and set the right pickup position. Lower INTENSITY a little.
It is much quieter or louder than my guitarAdjust OUTPUT. Try switching Volume knob changes level off to remove the loudness difference.
"Model failed to load"Reinstall 4real-audio. If it stays, contact support with your Windows or macOS version.
Standalone howlsUse headphones or mute input monitoring (chapter 10).

13. Uninstalling, support and legal

Uninstalling

Windows: Settings → Apps → 4real-audio → Uninstall. This removes the plugin, the Standalone app and this manual.
macOS: delete /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/4real-audio.vst3, /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/4real-audio.component, /Applications/4real-audio.app and /Library/Application Support/4real-audio.

Support

Website and account: https://3ffect.my.id. When you ask for help, tell us your plugin version (0.9.0), your operating system, your DAW and a short description of what happened.
